## Two different kinds of content in this KB — read this before citing anything from it

This knowledge base mixes two categories of material that must **not** be confused with each other:

### 1. Authority anchors (real, sourced, verifiable)
Nimrod's actual practical credentials in ecological land management — a 2014 Permaculture Design Course, a
formal ecological-agriculture study period, a multi-year biochar project, and a decade of hands-on garden-building
and teaching. These are **real biographical facts**, extracted verbatim from a single locked source file:
`/Users/nimrod/Documents/AOS_V5/nimrod-book/chapters/11_ERA_GARDEN_2013_2023.md`. They establish *why this domain
has practitioner authority behind it*, not a design methodology. Full citation detail: `04_CREDENTIALS_AND_PRECEDENT.md`.

**Do not** treat the biographical source as containing zone diagrams, guild tables, or swale specs — it doesn't.
It contains a transformation-arc narrative and a YouTube-corpus media summary. The design content below was not
mined from it.

### 2. Methodology (standard permaculture design theory, authored here)
Files `01`–`03` are **standard, public permaculture design theory** — the zone/sector model and guild concept
originate with Bill Mollison (*Permaculture: A Designer's Manual*, 1988) and David Holmgren (*Permaculture:
Principles and Pathways Beyond Sustainability*, 2002); water-harvesting/earthworks practice draws on the same
tradition plus general dryland/Mediterranean-climate greywater and swale practice. None of this was extracted
from Nimrod's book or bio — it is authored fresh for this project because no structured source existed. Where the
source file's real credentials (PDC 2014, Havat Adam ecological-agriculture training) plausibly back a *practice*
described in the methodology, this is noted inline as informal grounding — not as if the methodology text itself
were quoted from the source.
